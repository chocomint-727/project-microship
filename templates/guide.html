Explaining Genres

Action

High-energy scenes filled with intense battles, chases, and physical feats.

Adventure

Focuses on journeys to exciting locations and discovery.

Cars

Stories centered on vehicles, racing, and the automotive world.

Comedy

Lighthearted and humorous plots designed to make you laugh.

Dementia

Explores psychological and surreal themes, often unsettling.

Demons

Features supernatural beings, often involving battles between good and evil.

Drama

Emotional and character-driven stories with realistic conflicts.

Fantasy

Set in imaginative worlds with magical elements and creatures.

Game

Revolves around video games, board games, or virtual reality worlds.

Historical

Stories set in a specific historical period, often with authentic details.

Horror

Designed to scare and thrill, with elements of the macabre and supernatural.

Josei

Aimed at adult women, focusing on realistic relationships and emotions.

Kids

Simple, fun, and age-appropriate stories for younger audiences.

Magic

Focuses on spells, sorcery, and magical worlds.

Martial Arts

Centered around hand-to-hand combat and traditional fighting techniques.

Mecha

Features giant robots or mechanical suits, often in futuristic settings.

Military

Stories involving armies, warfare, and soldiers.

Music

Focuses on characters involved in music or the industry.

Mystery

Involves solving puzzles, crimes, or unraveling secrets.

Parody

Humorous takes on other works or genres.

Police

Stories about law enforcement and solving crimes.

Psychological

Explores the inner workings of the mind and emotional struggles.

Romance

Centers on romantic relationships and love stories.

Samurai

Features traditional Japanese warriors and their adventures.

School

Set in or around schools, focusing on student life.

Sci-Fi

Explores futuristic, technological, or scientific concepts.

Seinen

Aimed at adult men, often featuring mature themes and complex stories.

Shoujo

Aimed at young girls, focusing on relationships and emotional growth.

Shoujo Ai

Depicts romantic relationships between girls.

Shounen

Aimed at young boys, often action-packed and adventurous.

Shounen Ai

Depicts romantic relationships between boys.

Slice of Life

Depicts everyday life and relatable experiences.

Space

Explores outer space, futuristic technology, and intergalactic adventures.

Sports

Focuses on competitive sports and the challenges of athletes.

Super Power

Features characters with extraordinary abilities.

Supernatural

Explores phenomena beyond the natural world.

Thriller

Keeps you on the edge of your seat with suspense and tension.

Vampire

Features stories about vampires, often exploring immortality and bloodlust.

Yuri

Depicts romantic relationships between women.
